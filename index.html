Kansas and Missouri

Loan programs

Every program here is one Jason works with. Income limits, price limits and terms change, so the final word always comes from a full review of the buyer's situation.

MOKSLocation-based

USDA Guaranteed

$0 down
  • Home must sit in a USDA-eligible area. Many KC suburbs and outer-ring towns qualify.
  • Household income up to 115% of the area median.
  • Primary residence only. First-time status not required.
MODown payment help

MHDC First Place

4% assistance
  • Down payment help equal to 4% of the loan, forgiven after 10 years in the home.
  • For first-time buyers and veterans, or any buyer in a targeted area.
  • 640 minimum credit score. Works with FHA, VA, USDA and conventional.
  • Income and price limits apply by county. PHM is a preferred MHDC lender.
MOKSFHADown payment help

FHA Zero Down: Forgivable

$0 down
  • Covers the 3.5% FHA down payment with a second loan at 0% interest and no monthly payment.
  • Forgiven after 5 years if the home stays your primary residence and you don't fall 90+ days behind in the first 60 payments.
  • Sell, refinance or move out before year 5 and the full amount is due.
  • 640 minimum credit score (660 for manufactured homes).
  • No first-time buyer requirement and no income limit.
MOKSFHADown payment help

FHA Zero Down: Repayable

3.5% or 5%
  • Assistance of 3.5% or 5% of the price for down payment and closing costs.
  • Repaid as a second loan with a monthly payment on a 30-year schedule. The remaining balance is due in year 10.
  • 660 minimum credit score.
  • No first-time buyer requirement and no income limit.
MOKSLocation-based

Chase Community Lending

Neighborhood pricing
  • A conventional loan program for homes in Chase's designated Community Lending neighborhoods.
  • Eligibility is based on the property's address, not the buyer's income.
  • Benefits depend on the neighborhood's tier. Ask Jason what applies to the home you're looking at.
MOKSConventional

HomeReady and Home Possible

3% down
  • Conventional loans with reduced mortgage insurance.
  • Household income at or below 80% of the area median.
  • First-time status not required. Can pair with down payment assistance.
MOKSMilitary

VA

$0 down
  • For eligible veterans, active-duty service members and some surviving spouses.
  • No monthly mortgage insurance.
  • Veterans can also use MHDC First Place without being first-time buyers.
MOKSGovernment

FHA

3.5% down
  • Flexible credit guidelines for buyers rebuilding or just starting out.
  • Gift funds allowed for the down payment.
  • Pairs with MHDC First Place in Missouri.
MOKSLuxury

Jumbo

Over $832,750
  • For loan amounts above the 2026 conforming limit in the KC metro.
  • Options for buyers whose wealth sits in investment accounts rather than W-2 pay.
  • Jason works closely with wealth managers and financial advisors on structure.
Financing advantages

The CompetitivEdge℠ advantage

CompetitivEdge is Prosperity's program for buyers, sellers and agents. Instead of a standard pre-approval, eligible buyers shop with a fully underwritten loan commitment.

HomeSURE℠ Advantage1

An underwriter reviews your verified income, assets and credit before you shop, so your offer reads closer to a cash buyer's.

$30,000 On-Time Closing Guarantee2

If an eligible HomeSURE Advantage loan doesn't close on time, Prosperity pays:

$15,000to the seller
$15,000to the brokerage
Lock, Shop & Home3

Lock a rate for up to 90 days while you shop, or float and lock later.

REFIne Your Rate4

If rates drop, refinance with Prosperity within 3 years with no loan origination fee.

    Life outside the office

    I grew up a bit of a rolling stone, bouncing around OK, MO, NE and AZ with stops in Kansas City, Osage Beach, St. Joseph, Omaha (twice), Lincoln and Phoenix. Kansas City won in the end, and it's where we came back to raise our family. Today we live in Olathe, and you can hear us cheering for the Olathe West Owls.

    I've been married 24 years (my wife deserves most of the credit) and we have three kids, so our calendar is a color-coded map of volleyball, basketball and flag football. I spent years coaching youth sports, but these days you can find me in the stands with a lukewarm concession-stand coffee, trying my best to sit back and let the coach coach. On a free weekend, I'm with the Rotary Club of Johnson County Trailblazers, chasing a golf ball I probably sliced into the trees, or at Lake of the Ozarks with the family.

    Before mortgages, I spent 10 years in nonprofit development. I loved meeting people and solving problems, and lending threw in the math. Turns out I like the math. Fun fact: I was a DJ in college. I still take requests, they just sound more like "Can we close by Friday?" Go Chiefs, Royals, Sporting KC and KU!
