Where to put your profile so AI finds you

You can't submit anything to ChatGPT or Claude directly. They learn about loan officers by reading the public web, and for our industry that means Google, Bing, your company's site, the review platforms and NMLS Consumer Access. The job is to post these pieces where they read, with your name, company and NMLS ID identical everywhere.

Run this past compliance first. Most lenders require marketing review before anything goes public, and several of these destinations count as advertising. Send your compliance team the whole output, not just the bio.
  1. Check NMLS Consumer AccessGo to nmlsconsumeraccess.org and look yourself up. Your name, employer and license states there are the record AI tools and borrowers check against. Make everything else match it exactly.Anchor for everything
  2. Your company bio pageSend pieces 1, 2 and 4 to whoever maintains your profile page. The FAQ block is the piece most lender sites are missing.Uses pieces 1, 2 and 4
  3. Google Business ProfileIf your company allows individual profiles, go to google.com/business and paste piece 3 into the Description field. List your license states as service areas.Uses piece 3
  4. Bing PlacesChatGPT's web search leans heavily on Bing. Go to bingplaces.com and import from Google, or create one and paste piece 3.Uses piece 3
  5. Zillow lender profileClaim your profile in the Zillow lender directory and paste piece 2 into your About section. Zillow pages get crawled constantly.Uses piece 2
  6. Review platformsExperience.com, Google reviews, Zillow reviews. Paste piece 2 where there's a bio field, and ask happy borrowers to name the loan type and city in their review ("VA loan in Olathe").Uses piece 2
  7. LinkedInPaste piece 2 into your About section. Use the first sentence of piece 1 as your headline, with your NMLS ID in it.Uses pieces 1 and 2
  8. Your application site and socialPiece 1 as the intro on your application page and Facebook business page. Piece 4 makes good standalone social posts, one question at a time.Uses pieces 1 and 4
  9. Check your work in a few weeksIt takes weeks or months for AI tools to pick up changes. After 4 to 8 weeks, ask the five questions from piece 5 in ChatGPT and Claude with web search on, in a clean incognito window, and see whether you come up.Uses piece 5

Tip: the single biggest difference between loan officers who get named and those who don't is published, specific, program-level content. One honest page about down payment assistance in your state beats ten posts about rates.
